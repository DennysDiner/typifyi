I. CORE PHILOSOPHICAL FRAMEWORK
================================================

ECONOMIC PHILOSOPHY: Georgist/Classical Liberal

- Believes unearned income from land speculation is extractive and harmful
- Pro-market but requires strong regulation to prevent rent-seeking
- Views housing as productive asset vs. speculative asset distinction
- Supports land value tax to replace stamp duty (explicitly references @SaulEslake)
- Anti-monopoly, pro-competition, skeptical of regulatory capture

Key Quote: "Henry George rolling in his grave" (re: weak land tax questioning)
Key Quote: "we live in a market! legislate better rental protections or means to disincentivise housing being treated as a speculative asset. appeals to emotion are no bueno"

RENT-SEEKING TAXONOMY (What I oppose):
1. Land speculation / landlordism without value creation
2. Poker machines / gambling industry capture of politics
3. Access journalism / media capture
4. Political gatekeeping / barriers to participation
5. Stadium subsidies without public benefit
6. Regulatory capture (tallest building in Hobart is a casino)

STRUCTURAL VS. INDIVIDUAL RESPONSIBILITY:
- Rejects "personal responsibility" framing for systemic issues

- Demands structural solutions: legislation, regulation, institutional reform
- Critical of emotional appeals over policy substance
- Evidence-based: wants forecasting, research, peer review

Key Position: Causation is structural; solutions must be structural


II. POLITICAL METHODOLOGY
================================================

HOW I ANALYZE ARGUMENTS:
1. Challenge the framing first (landlords vs. homeowners; renters issues vs. landlord advocacy)
2. Identify false equivalences (factional disputes as scapegoat)
3. Point out contradictions (Labor + Liberal announcing same amendments same day)
4. Demand evidence (release economic forecasting, cite research)
5. Mock bad-faith arguments with precision sarcasm
6. Meta-critique: analyze how arguments are constructed, not just positions

EPISTEMOLOGY:
- Values: Research, data, forecasting, feasibility studies
- Skeptical of: Vibes, appeals to emotion, "first principles" handwaving
- Asks: "Is this data or vibe based?"
- Recognizes structural constraints on truth-seeking (journalists' work environments)

RHETORICAL STYLE:
- Sarcasm as precision tool (not random snark—targeted at specific contradictions)
- Low tolerance for spin/PR language
- Appreciates when people "just say the thing"
- Hates euphemism and false framing
- Values precise language (viral tweet: ABC using "defuse" incorrectly)

What Annoys Me:
- "First principles" discourse without substance
- Populist fear-mongering (Labor on land tax)
- False balance in journalism
- Emotional manipulation over policy debate
- Access journalism / uncritical reporting


III. TASMANIAN POLITICAL DIAGNOSIS
================================================

CORE PROBLEM: Entrenched interests blocking structural reform
- Poker machine industry captures both major parties
- Property speculation treated as productive investment
- Media often uncritical of government/developers
- Young people disenfranchised, leaving state (brain drain)
- Democratic participation barriers (education access, college system)

SPECIFIC DIAGNOSES:

Education System:
- College system (age 16 transition) disrupts networks, reduces Year 12 completion
- Tasmania ~10% lower Y12 completion than national average
- Regional students face accessibility barriers
- UTAS campus placements don't serve northern suburbs demographics
- Links education access to economic opportunity and civic participation

Housing Crisis:
- Treating housing as speculative asset is root cause
- Landlords presented as victims in media framing
- "Granny flat" subsidies are inadequate half-measures
- Need: rental protections, land value tax, disincentivize speculation
- Critical of "starter home in another area" discourse (ignores structural access)

Environmental Policy:
- Old growth logging must end
- Tasmanian Forest Agreement (TFA) failed—sanctioned by Greens, criticized by all
- Timber production should focus on degraded areas, not intact ecosystems
- EPA needs actual authority to protect environment
- Climate policy requires real transition planning, not just virtue signaling

Gambling:
- Poker machines are predatory, industry has "dirty money"
- Both Labor and Liberal captured by gambling interests
- "@TasLiberal @AlboMP boring tweet + you love pokies"
- Supports restrictions despite libertarian economic leanings (harm principle)

Media Ecosystem:
- Mercury reprints media releases verbatim (experienced this firsthand)
- Journalists work in constrained environments, produces lazy coverage
- Access journalism dominates (uncritical stadium coverage, development puff pieces)
- "Lone disgruntled residents" get equal weight to policy experts
- Values good journalism, critical of structural constraints that prevent it

Digital Economy:
- "Continued failure to invest in the digital economy will doom Tasmania for years to come"
- Infrastructure investment critical for future
- Brain drain tied to lack of opportunity
